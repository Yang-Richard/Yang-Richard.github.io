# 🥚 Todo Board Easter Eggs Guide

Welcome to the secret world of Todo Board! This app contains multiple hidden easter eggs for the curious and playful. Here's your complete guide to finding them all.

---

## 🎮 The Konami Code
**How to trigger:** Press the arrow keys and letters in this exact sequence: ↑ ↑ ↓ ↓ ← → ← → B A

**What happens:**
- The entire page gets a rainbow color-shifting effect
- Shows celebration message: "🎉 KONAMI CODE ACTIVATED! You found the secret! 🎮✨"
- Increments your easter egg counter

**Requirements:** Must not be typing in any input field

---


## 🛩️ Barrel Roll Command
**How to trigger:** Type "do a barrel roll" (case insensitive) in the main todo input field

**What happens:**
- The entire page spins 360 degrees in 1 second
- Shows message: "🛩️ BARREL ROLL COMPLETE! 🌀"
- The phrase is automatically removed from the input field
- Increments your easter egg counter

**Note:** This is a reference to the classic Google search easter egg and Star Fox video game

---

## 🤔 Productivity Meta-Joke
**How to trigger:** Type "be productive" (case insensitive) in the main todo input field

**What happens:**
- Shows meta-commentary: "🤔 Meta-productivity detected! You added 'be productive' to your todo list... to be more productive! 🔄"
- After 2 seconds, automatically replaces "be productive" with "actually do the thing"

---

## 📊 Secret Statistics
**How to trigger:** Visit the Settings panel and view your statistics

**What happens:**
- Random fake/funny statistics appear as notifications:
  - "Times you've stared at this screen: [random number]"
  - "Productivity level: [random]% (probably higher than you think)"
  - "Coffee required: [random] cups"
  - "Procrastination score: [random]/100"
  - "Tasks you forgot to add: Approximately [random number]"
  - "Secret hidden features found: [your actual count]"
  - "Time until you reorganize this list: [random] minutes"

**Note:** The easter egg counter shown is actually tracked and persistent

---

## 💬 Rotating Secret Placeholders
**How to trigger:** Leave the main todo input empty and wait

**What happens:**
- The placeholder text temporarily changes to funny alternatives:
  - "Add a task (or don't, I'm not your boss)"
  - "What needs doing today? Besides procrastinating..."
  - "Enter task here (it won't judge you)"
  - "Your task goes here ✨"
  - "Type something productive... eventually"
  - "Task idea: Actually use this todo app"
  - "What's on your mind? (besides lunch)"
  - "New task (this one will definitely get done)"
  - "Add task here (narrator: they did not add a task)"

---

## 🎵 The Original Easter Egg: Rick Roll
**How to trigger:** Go to the Notes panel and clear all content (or start with empty notes)

**What happens:**
- The placeholder text shows the complete lyrics to "Never Gonna Give You Up" by Rick Astley
- Classic rickroll in text form!

**Developer note:** This was the inspiration for all other easter eggs in the app

---

## 📈 Easter Egg Counter System
**How it works:**
- Each discovered easter egg increments a counter stored in browser localStorage
- Current count occasionally appears in the Secret Statistics
- Persists between app sessions
- Key: `easterEggsFound` in localStorage

**Total discoverable easter eggs:** 5 (Konami, Barrel Roll, and the 3 others that increment the counter)
